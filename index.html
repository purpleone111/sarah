I'MSARAH

Sarah Sheikh is a Bengaluru/Goa-based stand-up comedian, live performer, and creative producer whose work sits at the intersection of comedy, character performance, and cultural commentary.

With roots in the production side of the comedy scene at Underground Comedy Club, Sarah brings a strong understanding of both stagecraft and audience dynamics into her performances. Over the past year, she has performed across major comedy circuits in Bangalore, Mumbai, and Goa, steadily building a voice that is both irreverent and distinct.

DRAG KING

A defining aspect of her performance style is experimentation with form and identity.

She is the first Indian stand-up comedian to perform in a drag king persona, Chacha Cheech: a bold, exaggerated character that blends stand-up with theatrical satire, often engaging with themes of sexuality, masculinity, and social contradiction.

She also has a character based on local Bangalore boys, called Sheikh Potta. He is an outspoken, "porki" guy who is blissfully unaware of the chaos around him. His day is fulfilled with a Sulaimani or crushing on his new neighbour, Fatima.
He's also a proud Kannadiga and claims he can buy the Champagne region of France for his lady love
